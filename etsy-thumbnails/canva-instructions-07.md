# Product 7 — POD Mockup Bundle: Delivery Instructions

## What You Have
`sellable-07-mockups.html` — organized gallery of 50+ mockup scenes across 8 product categories with download buttons. You need to replace the placeholder download actions with real mockup files.

## How to Get Real Mockup Files (Free & Paid)

### Free Sources
- **Placeit.net** — free tier has limited mockups, but enough to start
- **Mockupworld.co** — large library of free PSD mockups
- **Freepik.com** — search "mockup" → filter Free → download PSD/PNG
- **Smartmockups.com** — free tier, good lifestyle scenes
- **Pixeden.com** — free PSDs, good quality

### Paid (Worth It for Volume)
- **Creative Market** — buy mockup bundles ($15-40, unlimited commercial use)
- **Envato Elements** ($16.50/mo) — unlimited mockup downloads, full commercial license
- **Placeit** paid plan — unlimited mockups rendered in browser, no Photoshop needed

### Generate Your Own with Canva
1. Canva → search "mockup" in templates
2. Choose a t-shirt/mug/poster template
3. Replace placeholder graphic with your design area
4. Share as Template link → buyers can swap in their own design

### Reaching the 500+ Claim
- 50 base scenes × 10 color backgrounds each = 500 variants
- Add each mockup in both light and dark background = 100 × 2 = 200
- OR: collect 100 unique mockups + offer 5 text overlay versions of each = 500
- Every method works — be straightforward in your listing description

## How to Use in Canva (include in your README)
1. Upload mockup PNG to Canva
2. Add your design as a new element on top
3. Adjust size and position to fit the print area
4. Use "Effects → Remove Background" if needed
5. Download → PNG with transparent background → upload to Etsy listing
